Modular End Effector System

Attached to our differential system is as fully modular end effector system. Currently attached is our largest gripper, which operates off of a rack and pinion system.

Belt Driven Differential

We designed a belt driven differential system to reduce the moment at the end of the arm; thus letting us maximize the possible payload of the arm.

UBC Rover's 6 DOF Robotic Arm

Participating in URC & CIRC, the Top Rover Competitions, this is UBC's Premier Robotics Design Team's Robotic Arm.

Full Extension

At full extension our arm is able to carry 5kg, while mounted on our rover.
